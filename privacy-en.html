Emres Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Emres is an Android app that reads delivery offers on a courier's screen and evaluates them for earnings, time and route. It is developed by, and this policy is the responsibility of, Emre Bülent. Contact: emresjourney03@gmail.com.

Emres is not affiliated with or endorsed by Uber, DoorDash or any other delivery platform.

1. Data we process

  • Screenshots: only when you tap the floating button, a single image of the current screen is taken with Android's screen-capture permission and the offer card is read on the phone (text recognition). There is no continuous recording.
  • Location: for the route origin and to detect arrival and delivery. While a shift is open it is also used while another app is in front (background). No background location is collected while no shift is open.
  • Physical activity: to tell walking from riding at a stop.
  • Delivery information: offer amounts, restaurant names, delivery areas, times, accept/decline decisions and shift earnings.
  • Settings: your platforms, vehicle type and preferences.

We do not ask for your name, phone number, email or any account. There is no sign-up.

2. Where data is kept

This data is stored on your phone, in the app's private storage. Emres has no server that collects it, and nothing is sent to us automatically. The app contains no advertising, analytics or third-party tracking.

Data leaves the phone only if you use "export" and choose the destination yourself (for example Drive or email). In the beta, an exported pack may contain screenshots, location history and addresses; you are asked to confirm before every export.

3. Third-party services

  • Google Maps Platform (Routes, Places, Geocoding): stop coordinates are sent to Google for route times, and restaurant names for restaurant locations. Delivery addresses are not sent to Google as text. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Google Play Integrity: to check that the app is an unmodified copy installed from Play.
  • GitHub: on first setup, the road-map file for your vehicle is downloaded from GitHub, which may see your IP address.
  • Anthropic (optional): only if you enter your own API key in settings, the text you choose to analyse is sent to Anthropic. Off by default.

4. Retention and deletion

Data stays on your phone until you delete it. Settings → Delete all my data removes every record, and uninstalling the app does the same. We hold no copy of your data. If you sent us a pack yourself, it is deleted within 30 days of an email request. Details: Delete my data.

5. Permissions

  • Screen capture (MediaProjection): to read the offer screen, only when you tap.
  • Display over other apps: the floating button and short prompts.
  • Location (including background): routing and arrival detection, only while a shift is open.
  • Physical activity: to tell walking from riding.
  • Notifications: shift status and reminders.

6. Children

Emres is not intended for anyone under 18.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, this page and its date are updated.
